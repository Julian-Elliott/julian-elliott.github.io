The Operator

Julian Elliott is a data analyst and automation specialist with 14+ years of turning messy operational reality into measurable outcomes. He builds pipelines, dashboards and robots-that-do-paperwork; by night the same instincts get pointed at solar panels, home servers and anything else in the house that can be made to emit telemetry.

He is currently reading for an MSc in Data Analytics at the University of Huddersfield, and is a GitHub Developer Program member with a Pull Shark ×3, Pair Extraordinaire ×3, Quickdraw and, yes, a YOLO badge to his name.

The Homelab

Beyond the public repositories sits a quietly humming rack: containerised services, self-hosted analytics, and a Home Assistant installation that knows more about the house than its occupants do. IoT sensors report in; Docker and Kubernetes keep the whole thing honest; a Badger 2040 e-ink badge displays whatever the pipeline decides is important today.
